Description:
A little forest. A moment of your own.

Enter Paper Garden and meet a butterfly, bird, fish, rabbit, fox and deer, all crafted from paper. All six companions are available to display from the start, without completing a puzzle. Choose a companion, adjust its position and size, and switch between Amber, Dusk and Moon lighting to arrange your little forest.

Tap your companion to say hello, watch it unfold, and enjoy gentle background music. The garden has no move limits, timers or win conditions. Your arrangement is saved on this device for your next visit.

For a little challenge, open the separate level book. Swap neighboring patterns and fold the paper to bring distant patterns together. Explore 12 fixed puzzles with fold previews, free undo and two-stage hints. Every two completed levels add a finished creature to your puzzle collection. Earned collection progress is separate from the freely available garden companions.

A deep forest palette, warm light and paper textures accompany English and Simplified Chinese interfaces. Everything works offline without an account. Adjust sound, haptics and motion independently, with tap controls, VoiceOver labels and text-size support.

Your garden arrangement and puzzle progress stay on this device. Cross-device sync is not included in this release.

What's new:
The first release includes Paper Garden, six freely available paper companions, three lighting moods, position and size controls, greeting and unfolding animations, plus 12 optional puzzles and a separate puzzle collection.
