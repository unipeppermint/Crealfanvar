Name: Paperkin
Subtitle: Fold paper. Match patterns.
Keywords: origami,puzzle,match,paper,fold,offline,relaxing,creatures,logic
Suggested category: Games / Puzzle
Pricing baseline: Free. No purchases, advertisements, accounts or energy limits.

Description:
Discover a different way to match on a small sheet of paper.

Swap adjacent patterns, or fold along a crease to bring distant patterns together. Plan around overlapping layers and complete each goal within the move limit.

Explore 12 fixed puzzles and unfold six paper creatures, unlocking one after every two levels. Meet a butterfly, bird, fish, rabbit, fox and deer, each with three stages of creation and an unfolding animation to revisit.

There is no timer and no random refill. Take your time, undo a move, ask for a two-stage hint, and continue where you left off. Every puzzle works offline, with no account required.

Available in English and Simplified Chinese. Patterns use both shape and color. Tapping can replace dragging, and sound, haptics and motion have separate controls.

Progress stays on this device. Cross-device sync is not included in this release.

What's new:
The first release includes all 12 puzzles, six paper creatures, fold previews, undo, hints and local progress saving.
