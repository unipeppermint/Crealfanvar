Description:
A little forest. A moment of your own.

Enter Paper Garden and meet a butterfly, bird, fish, rabbit, fox and deer, all crafted from paper. All six companions are available to display from the start, without completing a puzzle. Arrange up to three companions together, adjust each position and size, and add freely available paper trees and pebbles. Switch between Amber, Dusk and Moon lighting, and save three favorite arrangements to revisit.

Tap a companion for its own greeting: a flutter, a hop, a swish or a bow. Watch the paper unfold, and enjoy gentle background music. The garden has no move limits, timers or win conditions. Your arrangement is saved on this device for your next visit.

For a little challenge, open the separate level book. Swap neighboring patterns and fold the paper to bring distant patterns together. Explore 24 fixed puzzles with fold previews, free undo and two-stage hints. In the first 12 levels, every two completed levels add a finished creature to your puzzle collection. The next 12 offer deeper, multi-step folding challenges. Complete levels 16, 20 and 24 to earn a paper lantern, a quiet pond setting and a star garland for your garden. All six companions, basic props and three saved arrangements remain freely available without completing a puzzle.

Explore companion notes and three illustrated making stages in the collection, then revisit the two puzzles that made each creature. Completion records reflect your own journey.

A deep forest palette, warm light and paper textures accompany English and Simplified Chinese interfaces. Everything works offline without an account. Adjust sound, haptics and motion independently, with tap controls, VoiceOver labels and text-size support.

Your garden arrangement and puzzle progress stay on this device. Cross-device sync is not included in this release.

What's new:
The first release includes Paper Garden, six freely available paper companions, room for three together, three lighting moods, basic props, three saved arrangements, and individual greeting and unfolding animations, plus 24 optional puzzles and a separate puzzle collection.
