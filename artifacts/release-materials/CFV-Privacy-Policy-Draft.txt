折叠生物 / Paperkin 隐私说明草稿（尚未部署，不作为有效公开地址）

本应用无需账号。应用不会通过自己的代码收集或上传用户信息，不含广告、分析或第三方运行时 SDK。
花园布置（伙伴、光色、位置和大小）、关卡进度、收藏、最佳步数、当前对局、撤销历史和设置保存在本设备。应用不提供跨设备同步。设备自身的备份服务是否包含应用数据取决于系统及用户设置。
删除应用可能删除这些本地记录。应用本身不提供开发者访问玩家存档的通道。
核心玩法不联网。用户点击公开支持或隐私链接时，系统浏览器会打开所配置的网站，该网站需要另行披露其实际数据行为。

发布前补充：开发者或数据控制者真实名称、支持联系方式、政策生效日期、实际支持网站行为及适用地区条款。部署后将 HTTPS 地址写入 Info.plist 的 CFVPrivacyURL。

Privacy policy draft (not yet a public policy URL)
Paperkin does not require an account and does not collect or upload personal information through its own code. It contains no advertising, analytics or third-party runtime SDKs.
Garden arrangements (companion, light, placement and size), level completion, creatures, best move counts, the active game, undo history and preferences are stored on this device. The app does not provide cross-device sync. Device backup behavior depends on the operating system and the user's settings.
Deleting the app may delete these records. The app provides no channel for the developer to access player saves.
Core gameplay does not use the network. Opening a configured privacy or support link uses the system browser; the website's actual data practices must be disclosed separately.

Before publication, add the genuine developer/controller identity, support contact, effective date, website data practices and applicable regional terms. Deploy the policy and configure its HTTPS URL using the CFVPrivacyURL Info.plist key.
