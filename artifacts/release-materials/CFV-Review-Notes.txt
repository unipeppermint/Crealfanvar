App Review notes — draft for the final signed build

Paperkin is an offline paper-creature entertainment app with an ungated Paper Garden and optional folding puzzles. Requested primary category: Entertainment; secondary: Games / Puzzle. No login, purchases or external hardware are required.

From Home, tap Enter Paper Garden. All six companions are available immediately, independent of puzzle completion. Choose a companion, lighting mood (Amber, Dusk or Moon), left/center/right placement and size. The scene stays visible while the controls scroll. Tap the scene or Say hello for a greeting, or Unfold to replay the paper animation. Changes are saved locally and appear on Home. Garden use never grants or removes earned puzzle collection progress. Reduce Motion removes the greeting movement and unfolding animation.

For the optional puzzles, use Start Folding / Continue level or Level book on Home. Level 1 introduces matching; Level 2 introduces folding. Tap the crease button (for example Right 2 cols) or the dashed crease to preview the visible surface, then tap Confirm Fold. You can also drag inward from the paper edge. Previews do not spend moves. Only tiles on the top paper layer match, including the rule that blank paper covers lower tiles. The paper unfolds after resolution.

Hollow diamond marks identify the targets. The marks follow tiles during swaps. Clear all marked tiles to finish. There are no random refills. Undo is free and restores the previous board and move count. Hint first identifies an action type, then Show move reveals the exact action.

The release contains 12 levels in one level book and six collectible creatures. Complete two levels per creature. Unlocks occur at 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12. Every puzzle can be completed through the normal UI. No review-only gameplay exists.

Settings on Home include music, effects, haptics, reduced motion, language, offline help and privacy/support information. Local progress resumes after reopening the app.
